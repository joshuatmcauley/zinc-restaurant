Good to know

Getting in

Step-free entrance, accessible toilets, accessible parking and table seating throughout. Nobody has to be carried anywhere.

Bringing the family

High chairs, a kids’ menu and staff who are used to a table of six with three under-tens at it.

Allergies and diets

Gluten free, dairy free, vegetarian and vegan options. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will sort you out.

Parking

Free car park on site and plenty of spaces on the street. Riverside Park is a two-minute walk if you want one after.
