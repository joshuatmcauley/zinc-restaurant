When to ring ahead

  1. Sunday lunch

    Service starts at noon and the early-afternoon tables go first. This is the one worth booking a few days out.

  2. Friday and Saturday evening

    The kitchen runs the specials board and the room is busy. Walk-ins are welcome but a booking saves you standing at the door.

  3. Larger tables and occasions

    Birthdays, work nights out, christenings. Tell us how many and roughly when, and we will put something together.

  4. Midweek

    Tuesday to Thursday is usually easier, and it is when the two-course teatime menu runs. Still worth a quick call.

Tell us when you book

Anything that makes the night go smoother: allergies or dietary needs, a high chair, a wheelchair space, a cake you want brought out, or that one of you is driving and wants the kitchen to know.
